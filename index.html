01 / About

A bit about me.

I'm a software engineer at A-to-Be, working on mobility systems and helping modernize the services people rely on to get around.

I learned the hands-on way. At 42 School I rebuilt the classics from scratch: a Unix shell, an HTTP server, a raycasting engine. At SEA:ME I took that to mobility and automotive systems, building a Jetson Nano powered autonomous model car with lane segmentation, traffic-sign detection and a Qt instrument cluster.

On the side I build other projects, like BikefitSim, an open-source app that clones your bike fit and simulates it on any other frame.
